A Community Rooted in Wisdom and Peace

We are a community of individuals dedicated to cultivating inner peace, wisdom, and compassion for everyone—regardless of background, beliefs, or experience.

While our teachings are rooted in the timeless wisdom of the Buddha, and the ancient Theravāda tradition, our approach is non-religious, practical, and open to all. Whether you’re looking to reduce stress, improve mental clarity, or cultivate inner calm, our programs offer simple, time-tested techniques that integrate easily into daily life.

Our commitment to Mindfulness is unwavering, and we encourage everyone to explore the depths of their being through mindfulness meditation.

FAQ

Is there a dress code?

Modest clothing is recommended for both men and women. Wear what is comfortable for you.

What is the pricing for different activities?

All of our events and activities are completely free-of-charge. We are a charitable organization that runs on donations and offer all of our programming at no cost to you.

Can I sit on a chair instead of the floor?

Absolutely. Even though you may notice people sitting on the floor, we also have stools, cushions and chairs. Feel free to also bring your own favorite chair.

What should I wear?

Wear something comfortable and modest. Choose clothes you can relax in easily.

Is this a religious practice?

No, it’s a mindfulness practice open to all. While inspired by Buddhist teachings, it’s not a religious ceremony. Everyone is welcome, regardless of background.
